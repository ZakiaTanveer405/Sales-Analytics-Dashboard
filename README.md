# 📊 Sales and Profit Dashboard (Excel)
<br>

## 📌 Project Overview

The Sales and Profit Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze business performance across different years, product categories, customers, states, and months. It enables users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and make data-driven decisions through dynamic charts and slicers.
<br> 

## 🎯 Objectives

The dashboard helps answer important business questions such as:

What are the total sales and total profits?
Which product category generates the highest sales?
How does profit change over different years?
Which customers contribute the highest profit?
Which states generate the highest sales?
Which months perform the best?
How do different filters affect overall performance?

## 📊 Dashboard Features
### 1. KPI Cards

The dashboard displays two major KPIs:

Total Sales: $1,928,888
Total Profit: $247,961

These cards provide an instant overview of business performance.

### 2. Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard using slicers:

Year
Category
Month

All visualizations update automatically based on the selected filters.

### 3. Profit by Year

A clustered column chart compares yearly profits across:

Furniture
Office Supplies
Technology

This helps identify yearly trends and category performance.

### 4. Sales by Category

A funnel chart ranks product categories according to total sales.

Top-performing categories include:

Phones
Chairs
Storage
Binders
Tables
### 5. Customer Count

A donut chart shows customer distribution by year, making it easy to compare customer activity over time.

### 6. Sales by State

An Excel Map visualizes sales across U.S. states.

It quickly highlights:

High-performing states
Low-performing regions
Geographic sales distribution
### 7. Top 5 Customer Profit

A horizontal bar chart displays the customers generating the highest profits.

This helps identify valuable customers and potential business opportunities.

### 8. Sales by Month

A monthly sales chart identifies seasonal patterns and peak-performing months.

Highest sales months include:

November
December
September
## 📈 Key Insights
Technology products contribute significantly to total sales.
November and December generate the highest revenue.
A small group of customers contributes a large share of profit.
Sales performance varies across different states.
Interactive slicers allow detailed business analysis with a single click.
